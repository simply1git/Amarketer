# Grammarly vs ProWritingAid: Which AI Writing Assistant Wins in 2023?

Struggling to choose between Grammarly and ProWritingAid? Both tools promise polished writing, but their approaches differ significantly. After extensive testing, here's our detailed breakdown to help you decide.

## Quick Comparison: Grammarly vs ProWritingAid

| Feature               | Grammarly           | ProWritingAid       |
|-----------------------|---------------------|---------------------|
| Best For              | Quick edits, casual writing | In-depth analysis, long-form content |
| Grammar Checking      | Excellent           | Excellent           |
| Style Suggestions     | Strong              | Exceptional         |
| Plagiarism Checker    | Premium feature     | Premium feature     |
| Integrations          | Browser, Office, Mobile | Limited browser support |
| Learning Resources    | Basic               | Extensive           |
| Pricing Tier          | Free to $30+/mo     | Free to $20+/mo     |

## Key Features Compared

### 1. Real-Time Editing Experience
**Grammarly** shines with its sleek, non-intrusive interface that works everywhere you write. The Chrome extension provides instant corrections with colorful underlines (red for grammar, blue for clarity, etc.).

**ProWritingAid** offers more granular control with its "Reports" system. Instead of real-time flags, you run 20+ specialized analyses (e.g., sticky sentences, pacing) after drafting.

*Winner for speed*: Grammarly  
*Winner for depth*: ProWritingAid

### 2. Style and Tone Adjustments
Grammarly's tone detector helps tailor messages for different audiences (formal, friendly, etc.). Its suggestions tend to prioritize brevity.

ProWritingAid digs deeper into stylistic elements, identifying:
- Overused words  
- Sentence length variation  
- Passive voice overuse  
- Even "glue words" that weaken prose

*Winner*: ProWritingAid for authors, Grammarly for business communicators

### 3. Integrations and Workflow
Grammarly integrates seamlessly with:
- Gmail  
- Google Docs  
- Microsoft Office  
- Social media platforms  

ProWritingAid works best in its web editor or via direct Word/Scrivener plugins. Browser support is more limited.

*Winner*: Grammarly for most users

## Who Should Choose Grammarly?

✔ Professionals who need quick, everyday writing help  
✔ Non-native English speakers wanting instant corrections  
✔ Teams needing shared style guidelines  

## Who Should Pick ProWritingAid?

✔ Authors working on books/long manuscripts  
✔ Academics who need detailed style reports  
✔ Writers who want to improve craft (not just fix errors)  

## Pricing Breakdown

Both offer **free tiers** with basic functionality. Premium plans fall into these brackets:

|                | Grammarly       | ProWritingAid   |
|----------------|-----------------|-----------------|
| **Free Tier**  | Basic grammar checks | Basic grammar/style |
| **Premium**    | $30+/mo        | $20+/mo         |
| **Business**   | Custom pricing  | Not available   |

*Note*: ProWritingAid offers lifetime licenses (one-time payment), while Grammarly is subscription-only.

## Final Verdict

**Choose Grammarly if** you want:  
✓ Effortless, real-time corrections  
✓ Broad app integrations  
✓ Simple, polished writing  

**Pick ProWritingAid if** you prefer:  
✓ Deep stylistic analysis
