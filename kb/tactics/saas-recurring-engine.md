# High-Ticket Recurring SaaS Affiliate Engine

## Strategy Rationale

Unlike one-time physical product commissions (1%–4%), SaaS affiliate programs pay 30%–60% monthly recurring revenue (MRR) for the lifespan of the customer subscription.

### Key High-Margin SaaS Categories (2026)

| Category | Top Programs | Typical Payout | Average MRR / User |
|---|---|---|---|
| **All-in-One Funnels** | Systeme.io, GoHighLevel | 40%–60% Lifetime | $20–$180/mo |
| **Email & Newsletters** | MailerLite, Beehiiv, GetResponse | 30%–40% Recurring | $15–$90/mo |
| **AI Video & Media** | Synthesia, HeyGen, ElevenLabs | 20%–30% Recurring | $10–$60/mo |
| **Creator Platforms** | Kajabi, Teachable, Skool | 30% Lifetime | $45–$120/mo |

## High-Conversion Content Structures

1. **Free-Tier Limits Teardowns:** Focus on where the $0 free plan ends and why upgrading to Pro is necessary.
2. **Side-by-Side Tool Battles:** ("Systeme.io vs MailerLite", "Kajabi vs Teachable").
3. **Setup Guides & Templates:** Offer free downloadable templates/funnels that require the user to create a free account to use.
