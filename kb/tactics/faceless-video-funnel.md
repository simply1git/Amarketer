# Faceless Short-Form Video Content Pipeline

## Strategy Overview

Faceless short-form videos (30–60 seconds) leverage high algorithmic organic reach on YouTube Shorts, TikTok, and Instagram Reels to capture top-of-funnel attention without showing your face on camera.

## Script Architecture (Hook-Problem-Solution-CTA)

1. **0–3s Hook:** Interrupt pattern with a bold statement ("3 AI tools that feel illegal to know in 2026").
2. **3–15s Problem:** Highlight a friction point ("Stop paying $100/mo for separate funnel and email software").
3. **15–45s Solution & Proof:** Show 2–3 rapid feature highlights of the recommended tool.
4. **45–60s CTA:** Direct viewers to the link in bio/description for full tested breakdown on SoloStack.

## Voiceover Generation

Use `tools/make_voiceover.py` with free Microsoft Edge-TTS voices (`en-US-ChristopherNeural` or `en-US-JennyNeural`).
